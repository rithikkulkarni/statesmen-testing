# Hiring Plan — 2026

**Goal:** Build the founding team that ships the first version of the product and establishes the engineering culture.

**Company goal:** Be the best.

---

## Phase 1 — Founding Engineer (Now)

| Role | Agent | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| CTO / Founding Engineer | CTO (`cto`) | ✅ Hired 2026-06-18 |

**Why CTO first:** Technical architecture decisions made in the first 90 days are load-bearing. The CTO owns the stack, sets the pace, and keeps the lights on while the rest of the team is hired.

**CTO responsibilities (first 90 days):**
- Establish repo structure and dev tooling
- Define and document the tech stack
- Build and ship the MVP
- Set up CI/CD, observability, and deployment
- Keep the backlog moving

## Phase 2 — Design (When Product Direction is Locked)

| Role | Agent | Trigger |
|------|-------|---------|
| UX Designer | UXDesigner | After MVP scope is defined |

Design joins when there is a clear product direction and a real user to design for. No design-in-a-vacuum.

---

## Phase 3 — Growth (After First Users)

| Role | Agent | Trigger |
|------|-------|---------|
| CMO / Growth Lead | CMO | After product has ≥1 paying user or measurable traction |

Marketing and growth join only when there is something worth marketing. Hiring growth before product-market fit is wasteful.

---

## Hiring Principles

1. **Hire for the next 12 months, not the next 5 years.** Over-hiring early burns cash and creates coordination overhead before you have product clarity.
2. **Agents own outcomes, not tasks.** Every hire should be able to define success for their role without CEO input.
3. **No passenger hires.** Every agent must ship something real in the first sprint.
4. **Culture is behavior, not values.** Judge candidates on what they do, not what they say.

---

## Open Questions for the Board

- What is the company building? (Product/market definition needed to sharpen Phase 1 roadmap)
- Are there specific technologies or constraints to build around?
- What does "be the best" mean concretely — best at what, for whom?
